About KESB

A 100% Bumiputra specialist in telecommunications infrastructure across Malaysia.

Introduction

Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd (KESB) was incorporated on 9 September 2015 and is a 100% Bumiputra company. We deliver end-to-end telecommunications infrastructure — site acquisition, engineering, construction, installation, consultancy, project management and site maintenance.

On 14 July 2021, KESB was awarded the Network Facility Provider (NFP) licence by the Government of Malaysia.

Vision

To be the preferred telecommunications infrastructure support provider in the Southeast Asia region.

Mission

To exceed customer's expectations through focus on speed, harnessing of innovation and devotion to quality.

How we work

KESB is built on professionalism and rapid infrastructure deployment, while staying flexible to clients' changing needs. Our edge is delivering challenging sites — those with technical, legal, regulatory or logistical constraints — so telecommunications companies can focus on service delivery.

NFP License

On 14 July 2021, Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd was awarded the Network Facility Provider (NFP) Individual Licence (Licence No: NFP/I/2000/476) by the Ministry of Communications and Multimedia Malaysia. The licence is valid from 14 July 2021 to 13 July 2031.
