About KESB

Background, corporate structure and company information.

Introduction

Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd ("KESB") was incorporated on 9 September 2015 in Malaysia and is a 100% Bumiputra company. KESB is a specialist in telecommunications infrastructure projects providing end-to-end solutions including site acquisition, engineering, construction and installation.

KESB also provides consultancy, project management and site maintenance services for telecommunications projects throughout Malaysia.

On 14 July 2021, Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd was awarded the Network Facility Provider (NFP) license by the Government of Malaysia for its track record and future role in the telecommunications industry.

Vision

To be the preferred telecommunications infrastructure support provider in the Southeast Asia region.

Mission

To exceed customer's expectations through focus on speed, harnessing of innovation and devotion to quality.

Corporate Structure

Utmost professionalism and commitment to fulfilling clients' needs make up the foundation upon which KESB's success is built upon. In its early days, clients, landowners and other stakeholders alike were frequently amazed by KESB's professional approach to all its endeavors, despite its size.

In essence, KESB has built its success since its inception through its focus on supporting its clients in the telecommunications industry in rapidly deploying infrastructure whilst maintaining flexibility in ensuring that their dynamic needs are met.

Over the years, KESB has built its competitive advantage in the acquisition and establishment of challenging sites that are often plagued with technical, legal, regulatory, bureaucratic and logistical difficulties. This has, in turn, encouraged KESB to continually pursue innovation whilst sustaining the flexibility required resolving the many challenges at hand.

Today, KESB is well equipped to meet challenges presented whilst ensuring that its clients, the telecommunications companies, are able to focus on service delivery to mobile users.

NFP License

On 14 July 2021, Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd was awarded the Network Facility Provider (NFP) Individual Licence (Licence No: NFP/I/2000/476) by the Ministry of Communications and Multimedia Malaysia. The licence is valid from 14 July 2021 to 13 July 2031.
