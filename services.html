Our Services

Telecommunications infrastructure and fiber optic network facility services across Malaysia.

Telecommunications Services

Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd has been involved in a variety of telecommunication services, particularly telecommunication facility services. It has developed, constructed and managed many telecommunications infrastructure projects.

It is KESB's pledge to provide the finest telecommunications infrastructure installation, construction and maintenance services that will surpass the growing communication needs of the region.
