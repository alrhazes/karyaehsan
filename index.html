About KESB

Karya Ehsan Sdn Bhd ("KESB") was incorporated on 9 September 2015 in Malaysia and is a 100% Bumiputra company. KESB is a specialist in telecommunications infrastructure projects providing end-to-end solutions including site acquisition, engineering, construction and installation.

KESB also provides consultancy, project management and site maintenance services for telecommunications projects throughout Malaysia. KESB's skill and strong principles have gained trust from many service providers including incumbent telecommunications service providers.
