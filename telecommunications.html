Telecommunications Infrastructure

Site acquisition, consultancy, engineering, construction and maintenance for telecom tower facilities across Malaysia.

Structures

KESB develops, constructs and manages telecommunications infrastructure — from site acquisition through to ongoing maintenance. We deliver lattice towers, monopoles, lampoles and related site facilities for wireless connectivity.

Our edge is challenging sites with technical, legal, regulatory or logistical constraints, so service providers can focus on network delivery.

  1. 1

    Site Acquisition

    • Acquire sites for major telecommunications service providers' tower facilities.
    • Close relationships with land and property organisations and highway concessionaires.
  2. 2

    Consultancy

    • Site surveys and site design.
    • Local authority liaison and permit submissions.
    • Legal documentation and dispute resolution with landowners or authorities.
    • Turn-key tower development planning and management.
  3. 3

    Engineering

    • Site surveys, design and equipment installation.
    • Supply and install mechanical and electrical facilities.
    • End-to-end turn-key tower development, delivered on schedule.
  4. 4

    Construction

    • Mobilise, construct and supervise site facility implementation.
    • Supply of structure components for installations.
    • Power systems for site facilities, including renewable energy.
    • Knowledge, equipment and manpower to construct any size or type of tower.
  5. 5

    Maintenance

    • Operations and maintenance of structure sites, including emergency response.
    • Site landscape maintenance.
    • Complete site audits tailored to each customer.

Also offering fiber optic route access, project management and data centre interconnect.
