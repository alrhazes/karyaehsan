Our Expertise

We leverage our strengths in our team of experienced key professionals from various ICT industries who have taken us into various fields of expertise and functions in the telecommunication industry.

KESB prides itself in its capability to coordinate and manage complex, challenging and, in some cases, unstructured situations in delivering swift, innovative, quality and professional services to its clients. Project Management Civil Engineering Mechanical Engineering Electrical Engineering Architecture Quality Management Legal Finance

Current Activities

In brief, our expertise lies in end-to-end network infrastructure rollout which entails the following services:

  1. i. Site surveys — technical and non-technical.
  2. ii. Authority's liaison.
  3. iii. Submission for approvals of local authority.
  4. iv. Site design and consultancy.
  5. v. Site construction, supervision and mobilization.
  6. vi. Supply of Portable BTS, Monopole Towers, Monopole Trees and other type of structures.
  7. vii. Turnkey development contractor.
  8. viii. Equipment installation.
  1. ix. Mechanical and electrical works.
  2. x. Renewable energy implementation such as solar power.
  3. xi. Operations and maintenance of structure sites.
  4. xii. Landscape maintenance.
  5. xiii. Fiber optic route access applications and project management.
  6. xiv. Legal document management.
  7. xv. Dispute resolution with owners/authorities.
