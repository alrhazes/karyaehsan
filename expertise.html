Our Expertise

Multi-disciplinary capability for swift, professional telecommunications delivery.

We coordinate complex, often unstructured site conditions and deliver quality work on schedule. That takes skill across several disciplines:

Project Management Civil Engineering Mechanical Engineering Electrical Engineering Architecture Quality Management Legal Finance

What we deliver

End-to-end network infrastructure rollout, grouped by capability:

Site surveys & design

Technical and non-technical site surveys, site design and consultancy.

Authority & legal

Authority liaison, local-authority submissions, legal document management and dispute resolution.

Construction & structures

Site construction, supervision and mobilisation. Supply of portable BTS, monopole towers, monopole trees and other structures.

Turnkey & M&E

Turnkey development contracting, equipment installation, mechanical and electrical works, and solar / renewable energy.

Fiber optic rollout

Fiber optic route access applications and project management across Malaysia.

Operations & maintenance

O&M of structure sites and landscape maintenance after handover.
